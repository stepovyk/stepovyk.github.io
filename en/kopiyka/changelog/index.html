What’s new in Kopiyka

The app’s version history: what changes with every update.

Kopiyka checks for updates itself and offers to install the new version. You can always download the latest APK here.

  1. Version 1.1.0

    October 8, 2026

    Shared budget, automatic bank records and an assistant everywhere

    • Shared budget: connect your phones or budget together with your family. Transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and recurring payments appear on every device in the space within seconds. Everything is end-to-end encrypted — not even we can read your data. Invite with a link and keep the recovery key in Google Password Manager.
    • Automatic bank records: payments and income from PrivatBank and other banks record themselves from their notifications — into the account you chose for each bank, with a category based on the merchant. Notifications are read only on your phone and never sent anywhere.
    • Share a receipt photo or a bank message to Kopiyka from any app and it is recognised right away.
    • “Add by voice” — a new shortcut on the app icon.
    • The assistant is now everywhere: it sorts uncategorized records, answers questions about the transactions you found, explains spending in a category, suggests how to stay within a budget or reach a goal sooner, and offers a weekly review every Monday.
    • Changed a transaction’s category? Kopiyka offers to move the payee’s other records too.
    • Smoother navigation, tab and list animations.

  2. Version 1.13.0

    October 7, 2026

    A refreshed Kopiychyk and a smoother interface

    • The Kopiychyk mascot has a new look: a coin with an edge and rim, little arms and sparkling eyes. It waves hello, jumps for joy and ponders.
    • The home screen now shows today’s date and a greeting instead of the app name.
    • Screen transitions are smoother, home cards fade and glide into place, and income and spending totals count up to their new values.
    • The balance card now carries the Kopiyka mark.

  3. Version 1.12.0

    October 7, 2026

    Excel statements and a sharper quick add

    • Bank statements can now be imported from Excel files (.xlsx) as well as CSV.
    • Operations recognised by AI quick add can be corrected with a tap: change the amount, category or day.
    • Exchange rates and every currency list show full names, such as “US Dollar” or “Polish Zloty”.
    • The Dynamics chart in Analytics follows your choice of expenses or income, so a salary day no longer flattens daily spending.
    • The app is ready for Android 17, with updated components.

  4. Version 1.11.0

    October 7, 2026

    A new logo, a living Kopiychyk and a smarter AI

    • A new logo — a gold coin cut into pieces that form the letter K — with an animated launch screen on Android 12 and newer.
    • The Kopiychyk mascot comes alive: it breathes, blinks, cheers and ponders, and flips over when tapped.
    • The AI agent remembers the conversation between visits, accepts voice messages, can be stopped, and retries a failed request with one tap.
    • The agent does more: recategorises many operations at once, edits, pauses, pays or skips recurring payments, updates goals and answers with official NBU rates.
    • In quick add you can simply say “coffee 65, taxi 180” and the operations are recognised right away.
    • The amount keypad opens only when tapped, and a just-recorded operation can be undone from the main screen.

  5. Version 1.10.0

    October 6, 2026

    More reliable and more accurate

    • Skip a recurring payment right from Home.
    • Budgets are converted when the currency changes.
    • More accurate analytics.
    • More reliable monobank sync.
    • Protection from double taps and better contrast.

  6. Version 1.9.0

    AI agent and receipt scanner

    • AI agent: spending analysis and tips on how to save.
    • Receipt scanner: a photo of a receipt becomes a ready transaction.
    • The app tells you about new versions itself and updates in a few taps.
