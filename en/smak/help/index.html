Getting started

Smak runs on phones with Android 8.0 or newer. There is nothing to set up after installing: no account and no sign-up, so you can start adding recipes right away. The book, cooking mode, timers, meal plan and shopping list all work offline.

Smak is getting ready for Google Play. Leave your email and I’ll let you know as soon as you can install it.

Adding recipes

  • From a website. Paste a recipe link or open the site in the built-in browser – Smak finds the title, photo, ingredients and steps. The page is loaded straight from the website, without my servers.
  • From text. Paste a recipe copied from a chat or your notes.
  • From a photo. Take a picture of a cookbook page or a handwritten notebook – the AI assistant turns it into a tidy recipe.
  • By hand. Write your own recipe in the editor.

After an import you can always review and fix the recipe in the editor: websites format recipes in different ways, and small details are worth a quick look.

To find dishes faster, sort them into collections and categories, add tags, mark favorites and rate them. Without Smak Pro you can create up to three collections.

AI assistant

The assistant on Google Gemini works right away, with no keys or sign-up. 30 actions a month are free, 1000 with Smak Pro. Everything else works without the assistant, even offline.

What the assistant can do: turn a photo of a page into a recipe, answer questions while you cook, suggest ideas from what you have at home, adapt a recipe (vegetarian, lighter, kid-friendly), estimate calories and plan a menu from your recipes. It doesn’t just advise, it acts: it saves and edits recipes, plans the menu, adds to the shopping list and starts timers – and every action can be undone.

Privacy. Requests go through my assistant server to Google Gemini; neither the server nor Gemini stores photos or texts. See the Smak privacy policy.

Stay careful. AI answers can be inaccurate – double-check advice about food safety and allergens. If an answer is wrong or inappropriate, report it from the answer’s menu: the report reaches me and I will review it.

Cooking and timers

  • Cooking mode shows the recipe step by step in large text, and the screen stays on while you cook.
  • Timers. A time mentioned in a step starts as a timer with one tap. Timers keep running in the background: the notification shows the time left, lets you stop it or add a minute, and rings when the time is up.
  • Servings scale with kitchen-friendly rounding: “½ tsp”, not “0.48”.

Don’t see the timer notification? Make sure Smak is allowed to send notifications: Android settings → Apps → Smak → Notifications. Some phones restrict background work aggressively – if so, turn off battery optimization for Smak.

Meal plan and shopping

  • A weekly menu: breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for every day.
  • The shopping list builds itself from the recipes on your menu and is grouped by store aisle. Identical items are merged, and grams and milliliters turn into kilograms and liters.
  • With Smak Pro you also get weekly nutrition (calories, protein, fat and carbs per day and per week), menu templates and “repeat last week” in one tap.

Backups and a new phone

  1. On your old phone, open Settings → Create backup. Smak saves the whole book to a single .smak file – keep it wherever is convenient: Google Drive, email or your computer.
  2. On the new phone, install Smak and restore your book from that file.

Restoring only adds and updates recipes – nothing in your current book is deleted, so it is safe even on a phone that already has recipes.

If Google backup is turned on, Android may store the app’s data in your Google account. The assistant chat and the purchase status are not part of that backup: on a new phone, restore Pro.

You can share any single recipe as text or a beautiful PDF.

Smak Pro

The core app is free forever. Smak Pro is a subscription (59 ₴ a month or 399 ₴ a year with 7 days free) or a one-time lifetime purchase (999 ₴); prices in other countries are shown by Google Play. Pro adds:

  • 1000 assistant actions a month instead of 30;
  • five more color palettes and seven more home screen icons;
  • a PDF cookbook with a cover, contents and page numbers;
  • weekly nutrition: calories, protein, fat and carbs;
  • menu templates and “repeat last week”;
  • unlimited collections.

Payment goes through Google Play, and the app shows the price. If you chose a payment method that is confirmed later, Smak shows that the payment is pending and unlocks Pro as soon as Google Play confirms it.

New phone or reinstall. Sign in to Google Play with the same account, open “Smak Pro” and tap “Restore purchase”.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its rules; after a refund, Pro is turned off, and a subscription can be cancelled any time in Google Play. Collections you created beyond three are not taken away.

Tips are an optional way to say thanks: they don’t unlock anything and don’t affect how the app works.
